
# 📉 Customer Churn Analysis

An in-depth exploratory data analysis on a real-world Telco customer dataset, identifying the key drivers behind customer churn and uncovering actionable business insights to improve retention.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

Customer churn is one of the most critical business problems across industries — losing a customer costs far more than retaining one. This project systematically analyzes 7,043 Telco customers to answer: **who churns, why they churn, and what patterns predict churn.**

---

## 🔍 Key Findings

| Factor | Finding |
|--------|---------|
| Overall Churn Rate | 26.5% — 1 in 4 customers leaving |
| Biggest Driver | Contract type — monthly contracts have 43% churn vs 3% for 2-year |
| Most Vulnerable | New customers — first year is the most critical period |
| Internet Service | Fiber optic users churn at 42% |
| Demographics | Senior citizens churn at almost 2x the rate of non-seniors |
| Gender | No significant impact — males and females churn equally |

---

## ✨ What's Covered

- 📊 Overall churn rate distribution and class imbalance analysis
- 👥 Gender, age, and senior citizen impact on churn
- 📋 Contract type analysis — monthly vs one-year vs two-year
- 🌐 Internet service type and churn correlation
- 💳 Payment method and monthly charges vs churn
- 👨‍👩‍👧 Tenure analysis — identifying the most critical retention window
- 📈 8 business questions answered with data-driven insights

---

## 📊 Dataset

- **Source:** IBM Watson / Kaggle
- **Records:** 7,043 customers
- **Features:** 21 columns — demographics, services, account info
- **Target Variable:** Churn (Yes/No)
